# Product Requirements Document

## Product

TrustFirst Client Portal is a commercial SaaS platform for agencies, consultancies, and service businesses that manage client work across software, websites, branding, AI, marketing, ERP, automation, analytics, content, design, and operational transformation.

## Problem

Service organizations often coordinate client work across email, chat, spreadsheets, storage drives, project tools, and accounting systems. This creates fragmented requirements, unclear ownership, repeated status requests, unmanaged files, weak approval trails, and inconsistent client experiences.

## Goals

- Provide a single secure portal for all client-facing service delivery.
- Support multiple service lines without forcing every engagement into a software-project model.
- Standardize intake, requirements, approvals, files, notifications, and lifecycle tracking.
- Enable tenant-level configuration for commercial SaaS customers.
- Scale to thousands of client organizations and many active engagements per tenant.

## Non-Goals

- Do not prescribe a single delivery methodology.
- Do not require every client engagement to have tasks, sprints, or code artifacts.
- Do not replace specialist tools such as accounting, design, marketing automation, ERP, or source control.
- Do not implement backend workflows in the foundation phase.

## Core Users

- Platform Owner: operates the SaaS product.
- Tenant Owner: owns a subscribing agency or service company account.
- Internal Team Member: manages client relationships and delivery work.
- Client Contact: submits requirements, reviews progress, approves work, and accesses files.
- External Collaborator: participates in limited-scope work such as legal review, vendor delivery, or contractor execution.

## Core Capabilities

- Tenant management and client management.
- Client lifecycle tracking from lead to archived relationship.
- Engagement and project lifecycle tracking across different service categories.
- Requirement intake and structured discovery.
- File storage, versioning, and access controls.
- Role-based permissions.
- Approval and sign-off workflows.
- Notifications and reminders.
- Audit-ready activity history.
- Configurable metadata by service type.

## Service Categories

The platform must support at least:

- Software and SaaS development
- Website design and development
- Branding and identity
- AI consulting and automation
- Marketing campaigns
- Content production
- ERP and CRM implementation
- Data, analytics, and reporting
- Operations consulting
- Maintenance and managed services

## Success Metrics

- Reduced client status-update requests.
- Faster requirement intake completion.
- Higher approval traceability.
- Lower file retrieval friction.
- More consistent client onboarding.
- Tenant ability to manage many client types without custom code.

## Product Principles

- Client work is broader than software work.
- Tenant configuration should be preferred over one-off customization.
- Every major client-facing action should be traceable.
- Permissions must be explicit and least-privilege.
- The portal should make repeated operational work calmer, clearer, and faster.
