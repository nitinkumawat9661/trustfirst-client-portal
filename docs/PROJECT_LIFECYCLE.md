# Project and Engagement Lifecycle

## Purpose

The platform must support many forms of client work. An engagement is the commercial container; a project is an optional delivery structure used when the work benefits from milestones, tasks, or formal delivery tracking.

## Engagement Lifecycle

### Draft

Internal setup is in progress.

### Discovery

Requirements, assets, stakeholders, constraints, and goals are being collected.

### Proposed

Scope, budget, timeline, or service plan is awaiting approval.

### Approved

Client or internal approver has approved the engagement.

### Active

Delivery, consulting, campaign execution, implementation, or support is underway.

### Review

Work is ready for client review, internal QA, or stakeholder sign-off.

### Completed

Engagement objectives have been delivered or formally closed.

### Paused

Work is temporarily stopped.

### Cancelled

Work ended before completion.

### Archived

Engagement is retained for history but removed from active views.

## Project Lifecycle

### Planned

Project structure, milestones, team, and deliverables are being prepared.

### In Progress

Execution is underway.

### Blocked

Project cannot progress due to dependency, approval, access, payment, or decision delay.

### In Review

Output is awaiting review, QA, or approval.

### Change Requested

Client or internal reviewer requested changes.

### Completed

Project deliverables are accepted or closed.

### Archived

Project is retained for history.

## Service-Type Examples

- Branding: discovery, concept review, brand system delivery, asset handoff.
- Website: discovery, sitemap, design, build, QA, launch, support.
- AI Automation: assessment, workflow design, prototype, validation, rollout.
- ERP: discovery, configuration, migration, testing, training, go-live.
- Marketing: strategy, campaign setup, execution, reporting, optimization.
- Software: requirements, design, implementation, QA, release, maintenance.

## Lifecycle Rules

- Projects must belong to an engagement.
- Engagements can exist without projects.
- Deliverables can be attached to engagement or project scope.
- Approvals may gate lifecycle transitions.
- Lifecycle changes should create audit events.
